# Research Direction

Work within neural or variational few-body solvers for heavy multiquark Hamiltonians. The research problem is to improve variational resolution of AL1 tetraquark bound states while maintaining controlled sampling error and an explicit coupled-channel/color-sector treatment.

Acceptable improvements include better variational ansatz design in the same problem family, better sampling, optimization schedules, variance reduction, initialization, and more reliable artifact selection. A future scientist may replace DeepQuark with another neural or variational solver, but the submission must still solve the same AL1 few-body quantum problem and emit the same scientific quantities for the evaluator.

Do not switch to a table lookup, hardcoded paper results, a supervised model trained to imitate reported outputs, a direct fit to the evaluator outputs, or a different physical Hamiltonian for the named experiments. New Hamiltonians, much larger architectures, or unrelated many-body paradigms can be added as separate research extensions, but they should not replace the fixed targets used to compare against this reproduction.
