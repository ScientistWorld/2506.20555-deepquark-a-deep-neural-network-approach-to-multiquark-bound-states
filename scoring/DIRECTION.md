# Research Direction

Work within neural or variational few-body solvers for multiquark Hamiltonians. Acceptable improvements include better sampling, optimization schedules, neural ansatz refinements, variance reduction, and broader evaluation over the same AL1 problem family.

Do not switch to a table lookup, hardcoded paper results, a supervised model trained to imitate reported outputs, or a different physical Hamiltonian for the named experiments. New Hamiltonians or larger architectures can be added as additional experiments, but they should not replace the fixed targets.
